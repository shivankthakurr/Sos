# 10 Quick and Healthy Air Fryer Dinners for Busy Weeknights

In the modern household kitchen, few appliances have earned as much enduring praise as the versatile air fryer. By circulating superheated air at high velocity, this countertop convection wonder achieves that irresistible golden crispness of deep-frying with a fraction of the added oil.

Whether you are preparing a quick family dinner after work or meal-prepping for the week ahead, these wholesome recipes guarantee delicious results in under 25 minutes.

---

## 1. Crispy Herb-Crusted Salmon

Season fresh salmon fillets with olive oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and fresh dill. Air fry at 400°F for 9 minutes until flaky and fork-tender with perfectly charred skin.

## 2. Garlic Parmesan Cauliflower Bites

Toss cauliflower florets in avocado oil, grated parmesan cheese, and Italian seasoning. Cook at 375°F for 12 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through for even browning.

## 3. Stuffed Bell Peppers

Fill halved bell peppers with lean seasoned turkey, quinoa, black beans, and salsa. Top with shredded cheddar and air fry at 360°F for 14 minutes.
