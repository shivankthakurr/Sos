## 1. Malai Chaap: The Creamy Royale

For those who appreciate subtle elegance over fiery heat, the **Malai Chaap** is an undisputed masterpiece. Marinated meticulously in a velvety blend of fresh hung curd, heavy cashew paste, crushed green cardamom, and aromatic white pepper, this chaap is slow-roasted over gentle coals.

The result is a melt-in-your-mouth texture that is not just comforting, but truly sublime. It is finished with a glistening drizzle of pure desi ghee and roasted fenugreek leaves.

## 2. Afghani Chaap: The Smoky Herbaceous Delight

Steeped in traditional northwest frontier cooking techniques, our **Afghani Chaap** presents a delicate harmony of charred smoky edges and gentle herbal undertones. 

Marinated in garlic-infused cream, crushed coriander seeds, and mild green chillies, every skewer is roasted until golden amber. It strikes an exquisite equilibrium between richness and mild zest, making it a beloved family favorite.

## 3. Rogan Josh Chaap: The Kashmiri Royal Experience

Bringing the majestic aromatic spices of the Kashmir valley to vegetarian cuisine, the **Rogan Josh Chaap** is a bold, gravy-rich triumph. 

Infused with whole mace, rattan jot for that signature ruby hue, and slow-braised in a fennel-heavy gravy, this dish replicates the complex depth traditionally associated with royal wazwan dishes. Every bite is an explosion of warm, regal spices that tantalize the palate.

## 4. Tandoori Stuffed Chaap: The Ultimate Texture Contrast

If you are looking for an elevated sensory adventure, the **Tandoori Stuffed Chaap** offers innovation at its best. Hollow soya tubes are carefully stuffed with spiced cottage cheese, minced dry fruits, and chopped fresh mint before undergoing a fiery bath in our traditional red tandoori marinade.

Crispy and charred on the outside yet soft and luscious on the inside, it exemplifies the art of textural contrast.

## 5. Achari Chaap: The Tangy Pickled Punch

For adventurous palates that crave a sharp, tangy kick, the **Achari Chaap** delivers an electrifying experience. Drawing inspiration from centuries-old grandmother pickle recipes, the marinade features mustard seeds, fenugreek, fennel, onion seeds (kalonji), and cold-pressed mustard oil.

Each succulent piece bursts with pungent zest and aromatic fervor, making it an irresistible choice for spice lovers.

## 6. Haryali Chaap: The Fresh Herbaceous Sensation

Celebrating the verdant freshness of freshly harvested herbs, our **Haryali Chaap** brings together vibrant mint leaves, organic coriander, fiery green chillies, and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice.

The bright green marinade penetrates deep into every layer of the soya chaap, offering an invigorating, crisp, and refreshing bite that feels delightfully light on the stomach.

## 7. Masala Gravy Chaap: The Comfort Classic

No Indian culinary spread is complete without a rich, slow-simmered onion-tomato gravy. Our **Masala Gravy Chaap** features charred tandoori chaap chunks tossed in a thick, buttery reduction infused with roasted cumin and crushed kasuri methi.

It is hearty, soulful, and evocative of Sunday family feasts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What makes soya chaap a healthy dining choice?
Soya chaap is primarily composed of soybean flour and wheat gluten, making it exceptionally high in dietary protein and low in saturated fats. It provides an optimal amino acid profile for muscle maintenance while maintaining a satisfying, meat-like chew.
